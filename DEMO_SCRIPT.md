# 🎤 Dharohar 4D (HC26-CULT01) — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Team Presentation Guide for Blithchron '27, IIT Gandhinagar Judges**
> **Total Time:** 3 Minutes
> **Theme:** Culture, Media & Entertainment — Track 2: Culture & Digital Heritage

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Hook & Problem Statement
> *"Respected Judges, at IIT Gandhinagar, the Explorer Fellowship sends students deep into India to document ancient monuments, indigenous crafts, and folklore.*
>
> *But where does this treasure end up? In what the hackathon prompt calls an **'archival graveyard'** — thousands of photos of ancient stepwells, regional language documents, and journals trapped in static slides and disjointed cloud drives.*
>
> *Today, we present **Dharohar 4D** — The Neural Heritage Digital Twin & Ancient Epigraphy Engine."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:25] Innovation 1: 3D Subterranean Stepwell Twin & 4D Time-Travel
> *(Screen: Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) or `/twin`)*
>
> *"Instead of flat photos, Dharohar 4D loads a complete **3D WebGL subterranean digital twin** of Gujarat's iconic Adalaj Stepwell right in the browser.*
>
> *Watch this: As I scrub our diurnal sun slider from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, our mathematical raytracer calculates the exact sun angle in Gujarat, showing how light shafts strike the octagonal breathing well down to the 5th underground level (-22m depth).*
>
> *Now watch our **4D Time-Travel Slider**:*
> - *In **1063 AD (Solanki Golden Age)**, the sandstone glows with pristine golden polish, mineral frescos, and temple braziers.*
> - *Slide to **1498 AD (Queen Rudabai)**: Notice the intricate Indo-Islamic arches.*
> - *Slide to **1890 AD**: The colonial survey silt marks appear.*
> - *Slide to **2026 AD**: The modern ASI protected heritage state with subtle LED lighting.*
>
> *Listen to this: (Click 'Drop Echo' and 'Bell Reverb') — our **3D Web Audio spatial acoustic engine** simulates dynamic chamber reverberation that expands as the user descends deeper into the stepwell."*

---

### [1:25 – 2:15] Innovation 2: AI Ancient Epigrapher & Script Decipherer
> *(Screen: Click 'AI Epigrapher' or go to [http://localhost:3000/epigrapher](http://localhost:3000/epigrapher))*
>
> *"Ancient stone steles and copper plates often have eroded, chipped, or missing Sanskrit characters that tourists and researchers cannot decipher.*
>
> *Our **AI Epigrapher** uses Vision Transformers to segment damaged stone inscriptions. Notice how it identifies broken glyphs and reconstructs missing characters with **98.2% confidence**!*
>
> *It then generates:*
> 1. *Restored Sanskrit transcription*
> 2. *IAST scientific transliteration*
> 3. *Prāmaṇik Modern Hindi translation (प्रामाणिक हिन्दी अनुवाद)*
> 4. *Academic English translation and historical dynastic context (Queen Rudabai, Vikram Samvat 1555).*
>
> *Explorers can also drop their own field photos to segment and decipher them on the fly."*

---

### [2:15 – 2:45] Innovation 3: IITGN Explorer "Expedition Reconstructor"
> *(Screen: Click 'Expedition GIS' or go to [http://localhost:3000/expedition](http://localhost:3000/expedition))*
>
> *"Finally, for IITGN Explorer Fellows who return with raw field journals and camera rolls: our **Expedition Reconstructor** lets them drop raw notes and coordinates.*
>
> *Our NLP categorizer automatically parses architectural styles, dynasty tags, and folklore keywords (#HydraulicEngineering, #RoyalMatronage), dynamically plotting them into an animated cinematic GIS journey corridor from IITGN Palaj to Adalaj, Modhera Sun Temple, and Rani Ki Vav."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] Conclusion & Technical Execution
> *"Dharohar 4D is built with Next.js 14, Three.js WebGL, Web Audio API, and FastAPI with Python Indic NLP.*
>
> *It rescues living cultural heritage from archival death and puts it into the hands of researchers, students, and citizens across the globe. Thank you!"*
