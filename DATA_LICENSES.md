### Attribution Hygiene Rules
1. All AI image-to-3D models and procedural geometries are labeled **Reconstruction** to maintain historical distinction from survey-grade LiDAR point clouds.
2. Unseen user uploads are tagged **AI Draft Reading — Expert Review Recommended** to adhere to epigraphical research ethics.
3. No sacred deity idols or faces are autonomously generated; procedural generation is confined strictly to architectural elements (plinth, shikhara, gopuram, torana, columns, aquifers, and subterranean tiers).
