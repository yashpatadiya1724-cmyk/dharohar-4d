# Credits & Attributions — Dharohar 4D (HC26-CULT01)

Dharohar 4D is developed for **Blithchron '27, Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN)** under the Culture, Media & Entertainment / Open Innovation track.

---

## 1. Academic & Institutional Heritage Archives
* **IIT Gandhinagar Explorer Fellowship**: Field journals, photographic documentation, and geospatial trajectories from student explorer cohorts across Gujarat.
* **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)**: Epigraphical reports, monument preservation guidelines, and foundation records for Adalaj Ni Vav (N-GJ-2), Rani Ki Vav, and Modhera Sun Temple.
* **Epigraphia Indica & Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum**: Volume IX & historical inscriptions of the Western Chalukya, Solanki, and Vaghela dynasties.
* **Burgess & Cousens (1903)**: *The Architectural Antiquities of Northern Gujarat*, Archaeological Survey of Western India (Public Domain).

## 2. Indic Linguistics & Epigraphy
* **Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (DCS)**: Sanskrit morphological analysis and word segmentation (Dr. Oliver Hellwig).
* **GRETIL (Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages)**: Classical epigraphical Sanskrit shlokas and prashastis.
* **AI4Bharat**: IndicTrans2, IndicBART, and indic-transliteration resources.
* **Noto Sans Devanagari & Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit**: Open-source typography by Google Fonts & Tiro Typeworks (OFL).

## 3. 3D Graphics, Geometry & Solar Math
* **Three.js**: WebGL rendering engine, PCF soft shadow maps, and ACESFilmic tone mapping (MIT License).
* **NOAA Solar Calculator**: Standardized solar declination, equation of time, and diurnal altitude-azimuth formulas.
* **Poly Haven & ambientCG**: Public Domain (CC0) PBR sandstone, ashlar stone, and granite surface materials.

## 4. Multi-View Photography & Imagery
* **Wikimedia Commons**: High-resolution, multi-view photographic documentation licensed under CC-BY-SA 4.0 / CC-BY 3.0 / Public Domain.
* **Openverse**: Open-licensed cultural heritage photography aggregator.

## 5. Subterranean Acoustic Simulation
* **Web Audio API**: Real-time ConvolverNode impulse response synthesis with depth-calibrated RT60 decay (0.4s to 4.5s) and biquad lowpass filtering.
* **Pythagorean / Kharaharapriya Sanskrit Metrical Scale**: Traditional frequency ratios for Anushtubh meter harmonic synthesis.
